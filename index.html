Standard deviation is the square root of the variance, giving spread in the same units as the data. For independent variables the variances add — the result the lecturer flags as exam-critical.

Questions, answered.

Is it free? +

Yes — the core extension (custom speed, startup speed, time-saved tracking, the leaderboard, dark theme and all 5 languages) is free forever. The AI study toolkit is Premium: £1/month or £12 once for lifetime. You can also win Premium free by topping the weekly leaderboard, building login streaks, or referring friends.

How do the AI features work? +

The AI tools (notes, chat, flashcards and translation) are powered by Gemini and read your lecture's own captions, so everything stays grounded in your specific recording — answers even cite the exact timestamp you can jump to.

Can I export flashcards to Anki? +

Absolutely. Generate a deck from any lecture, then export it as a native Anki .apkg file (or a downloadable HTML deck) in one click.

Does it work on my university's Panopto? +

It works on any *.panopto.com and *.panopto.eu site, which covers the vast majority of university Panopto instances.
