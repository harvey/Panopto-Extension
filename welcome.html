How it works

Three steps, no account needed for the first one.

01Watch at any speed0.1× to 4× in the player, saved per lecture. Hold → to fast-forward at 2×, like YouTube.
02Sign in with Google onceUnlocks AI notes and flashcards. Free, no card, no trial to cancel.
03Click the icon on a lectureGenerate Notes, Generate Flashcards, or AI Chat. Notes and decks export to Notion, Word, PDF and Anki.

Everything runs from the lecture's own transcript, so it works on any Panopto video that has captions.
